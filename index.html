How can we study the mind?

Psychology is the study of the human mind and behaviour.

The mind itself is hard to study directly, because it doesn't always produce results we can measure. That's why psychologists look at both the biology of the brain and the behaviours we can observe, to build a clearer picture of how the mind works.
